Hidalgo County Future Leaders Council

Our mission

A hybrid, county-led civic leadership initiative created to invest in the next generation of community leaders — connecting young adults to local government, civic engagement, and regional opportunity, and working to retain, attract, develop, and empower young talent across the Rio Grande Valley.

Our vision

To help transform Hidalgo County into a premier destination for young people — cultivating professional opportunities, developing local talent, and enhancing quality of life — so the region keeps its next generation and attracts young professionals from across Texas.
